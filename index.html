Core Rules
Detachment Points Core Rule
A points pool (2 DP at 1,000pts, 3 DP at 2,000pts) spent on Detachments during list-building. Each Detachment grants a Force Disposition, which reveals your mission.
The Battle Round Core Rule
Both players take a turn. 5 phases per turn. Game ends after Round 5 or army is destroyed.
Battle-Shock ⚠ Core Rule
#1 most forgotten rule. Units below half strength roll 2D6 vs Leadership each Command Phase.
Objective Control Core Rule
Compare OC totals within 3". Battle-shocked units have OC 0.
Modifiers & Limits Core Rule
No roll can be modified by more than +1 or −1. Natural 1 always fails, natural 6 always hits/wounds.
Transports Core Rule
Embark within 3". Disembark in Movement Phase. Destroyed transport — bail out or risk mortal wounds.
Strategic Reserves Core Rule
Cannot arrive Turn 1. Set up within 6" of any edge, 8"+ from enemies. Left in reserve = destroyed.
Aircraft Core Rule
Must move ≥20" forward each turn. Can only fight/be charged by Fly units. Cannot Pile In or Consolidate.
Flying Core Rule
Fly models move over enemies freely. Must land outside Engagement Range. Measure through air on terrain.
Moving Over Terrain Core Rule
Terrain ≤2": free. Terrain >2": climb counting vertical distance. Cannot end mid-climb.
Mortal Wounds Core Rule
No save of any kind. Excess mortal wounds carry over to next model. Normal damage excess is lost.
Feel No Pain Core Rule
Roll D6 each time a wound is lost. Meet or beat the value: wound ignored. Only one FNP per wound.
Invulnerable Saves Core Rule
Choose armour OR invulnerable save per attack. Invulnerable saves are never modified by AP.
Aura Abilities Core Rule
Affect all eligible units in range. Model always in range of own Aura. Same Aura only applies once per unit.
Hazard Roll Core Rule
D6 per model, 1-2 fails = 1 mortal wound (3 if all Monster/Vehicle). Not an instant kill.
Core Concepts
Line of Sight Concept
True line of sight — model's eye view. Any part visible to any part = visible.
Engagement Range Concept
Within 2" horizontally and 5" vertically. In melee = cannot shoot, cannot Normal move.
Unit Coherency Concept
Within 2" of at least one model; within 9" of every model in the unit.
Attack Sequence Concept
Hit → Wound → Allocate → Save → Damage. Unmodified 1 always fails, 6 always hits/wounds.
Turn Phases
Command Phase Phase 1
Gain 1CP each. Take Battle-shock tests for units below half strength.
Movement Phase Phase 2
Normal move, Advance (no shoot/charge), Fall Back, or Remain Stationary.
Shooting Phase Phase 3
Declare all targets before rolling. Cannot shoot if Advanced, Fell Back, or in Engagement Range.
Charge Phase Phase 4
Declare charge, roll 2D6. Must reach all declared targets. Enemy may Overwatch.
Fight Phase Phase 5
Fights First units go first. Pile in 3", fight, consolidate 3". Alternate choosing units.
Terrain
Benefit of Cover Terrain
Worsens the attacker's BS by 1 — it's a to-hit penalty now, not a save bonus.
Ruins Terrain
Blocks all LoS, even windows. Cover worsens the shooter's BS by 1, not your save. Plunging Fire improves BS too.
Craters & Rubble Terrain
Infantry wholly on = Cover (-1 to the shooter's BS). Does not block line of sight.
Woods Terrain
Models wholly within never fully visible. Looking through = target never fully visible. Cover for hidden models.
Battlefield Debris & Statuary Terrain
Cannot end a move on top. Worsens shooter's BS by 1 for any model not fully visible through it.
Barricades Terrain
Infantry within 3" and partially hidden = Cover. Charge/fight across measured to the barricade itself.
Terrain Categories Terrain
Exposed, Light, and Dense — the three umbrella categories that govern movement and cover.
Terrain & Secured Objectives Terrain
When an objective coincides with a terrain area, the whole area is the objective. Secured stays held until contested.
Hills & Structures Terrain
Models can stand on top. No automatic cover. Better line of sight from height.
Hidden Terrain
Infantry/Beasts/Swarm in dense terrain who haven't shot are only visible within 15".
Obscuring Terrain Areas Terrain
If every LoS between two models crosses Light/Dense terrain, they can't see each other.
Solid Terrain
Dense terrain blocks LoS through any gap ≤3" off the ground — even windows and doors.

Battle Size
Each player secretly picks a Force Disposition card, then reveal simultaneously. Cross-reference against your opponent's choice — that gives your Primary Mission.
Shuffle the Deployment deck and draw one card. It describes where to place terrain, objectives, and where each side can deploy.
or choose manually:
Optional: Twist
Both players must agree to use one Twist card this battle.
Battlefields are 44" × 60". Use the suggested layout for your Deployment card, or build your own:
1–5: use the single central objective point shown on the Deployment card.
6: use two central objective points, 6" either side of the central point.
Roll off, then take turns placing terrain, winner first. For each objective point, place one terrain feature covering it — these become terrain objectives. Then place all remaining terrain.
Agree which battlefield edges match the Attacker's/Defender's edges on your Deployment card, then roll off — winner chooses who is Attacker and who is Defender.
Each player secretly picks Tactical (draw 2 each of your Command phases) or Fixed (pick 2 now, active all battle). Reveal simultaneously.
Both players secretly note down the following, then reveal simultaneously:
① Leaders
Which Leader units will start attached, and which Bodyguard unit each is attached to.
② Transports
Which units will start the battle embarked within Transport models, and which models they are embarked in.
③ Reserves
Which units will start the battle in Reserves (including Strategic Reserves). Aircraft must always start in Reserves.
Note: If a model has the Hover ability, you must declare at this step whether it will be in Hover mode for the battle.
Players alternate deploying units (excluding Strategic Reserves) one at a time, Defender first. All models must be set up wholly within their deployment zone. If you set up a TITANIC unit, skip your next turn to set up a unit.
Resolve any rules that let you redeploy units after both armies are deployed. Players alternate, starting with the Attacker. Units placed into Strategic Reserves here don't count towards the Reserves points limit.
Resolve pre-battle rules in order: Infiltrators (end of deployment roll-off), then Redeployment abilities, then Scout moves. The first turn player resolves first; alternate for abilities both players share.

Stratagems
Core Stratagems · grouped by when they're used
Either Player's Turn
1CP Command Re-roll
Core Stratagem Either Player's Turn
When
Any phase, just after you make an Advance, Charge, Damage, Hazard, Hit, Save, or Wound roll for a friendly unit/model, or a roll to determine the number of attacks generated with a weapon.
Target
That unit or model.
Effect
Re-roll that roll. If rolling more than one dice together, select one die to re-roll (except Charge rolls, which must be re-rolled in full).
Your Command Phase
1CP Insane Bravery
Core Stratagem Your Command Phase
When
Battle-shock step of your Command phase, just before you make a Battle-shock roll for a friendly unit.
Target
That unit.
Effect
That Battle-shock roll is automatically successful.
⚠ Cannot be used more than once per battle.
Your Shooting Phase
1CP Explosives
Core Stratagem Your Shooting Phase
When
Your Shooting phase.
Target
One friendly unengaged EXPLOSIVES/GRENADES unit that is eligible to shoot and did not make an Advance move this turn.
Effect
Select one EXPLOSIVES/GRENADES model in your unit, then select one unengaged enemy unit within 8" of and visible to it. Roll six D6: for each 4+, that enemy unit suffers 1 mortal wound.
Your Charge Phase
1CP Crushing Impact
Core Stratagem Your Charge Phase
When
Your Charge phase, just after a friendly MONSTER/VEHICLE unit ends a charge move.
Target
That MONSTER/VEHICLE unit.
Effect
Select one enemy unit engaged with your unit, and one model in your unit engaged with it. Roll a number of D6 equal to that model's T characteristic: for each 1, your unit suffers 1 mortal wound; for each 5+, the enemy unit suffers 1 mortal wound (max 6 mortal wounds per unit).
Your Fight Phase
1CP Epic Challenge
Core Stratagem Your Fight Phase
When
Fight phase, just after a friendly CHARACTER unit is selected to fight.
Target
That CHARACTER unit.
Effect
Select one CHARACTER model in your unit. Until the end of the phase, that model's melee weapons have the [PRECISION] ability.
Opponent's Movement Phase
1CP Rapid Ingress
Core Stratagem Opponent's Movement Phase
When
End of your opponent's Movement phase.
Target
One friendly unit in Strategic Reserves (excluding AIRCRAFT).
Effect
Your unit makes an Ingress move.
⚠ Cannot be used during the first battle round.
1CP Fire Overwatch
Core Stratagem Opponent's Movement Phase
When
End of your opponent's Movement phase.
Target
One friendly unengaged unit (excluding TITANIC units).
Effect
Your unit shoots using Snap Shooting — you can only target one visible enemy unit within 24", each attack only hits on an unmodified Hit roll of 6 (regardless of BS or modifiers), and you cannot re-roll Hit rolls.
Opponent's Shooting Phase
1CP Smokescreen
Core Stratagem Opponent's Shooting Phase
When
Start of your opponent's Shooting phase.
Target
One friendly SMOKE unit.
Effect
Until the end of the phase, each time an attack targets your SMOKE unit, or a unit that is not fully visible to the attacker because of your SMOKE unit, the target has the Benefit of Cover against that attack.
Opponent's Charge Phase
1CP Heroic Intervention
Core Stratagem Opponent's Charge Phase
When
End of your opponent's Charge phase.
Target
One friendly unengaged unit within 12" of one or more enemy units. A VEHICLE unit can only be selected if it is CHARACTER/WALKER.
Effect
Resolve a charge with your unit. Before making the charge roll, select one mode: Leap to Defend — can only select charge targets that made a charge move this phase; or Into the Fray (+1CP) — charge rolls greater than 6 are treated as 6, and you can select any enemy units within 6" as charge targets.
Opponent's Fight Phase
2CP Counteroffensive
Core Stratagem Opponent's Fight Phase
When
Fight step of your opponent's Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has resolved its attacks.
Target
One friendly unit that is eligible to fight.
Effect
Until the end of the phase, your unit has the Fights First ability and must be the next unit you select to fight.
